Definition Hierarchical Planning (Hierarchical Task Network - HTN Planning) is a planning approach in which a complex task is divided into smaller and simpler sub-tasks until they can be executed directly. Instead of planning at a very detailed level from the beginning, the problem is solved from high-level goals to low-level actions. Idea: "Divide a large task into smaller manageable tasks."
